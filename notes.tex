% .claude/rules/structure.md asks that new or rewritten text go inside

  \indrafttrue\color{draftcolor}%
  \captionsetup{labelfont={color=draftcolor},textfont={color=draftcolor}}}{%
  \par\noindent{\color{draftcolor}\rule{\linewidth}{0.6pt}}\par}

\section{Notes}
\label{sec:notes}
Working notebook. Keep child files in \texttt{notes/} and pull them in with
\verb|\input|; the dependency closure picks them up from the build record, so a
change in a child bumps this document and not the paper. Writing the path out
here would be read by latexpand as a real inclusion, so it is left implied.

This is a cite example\cite{example2024}: the bibliography is shared with
\texttt{main.tex}, only its rendering differs.

\begin{equation}
  \Braket{\psi | \bm{H} | \psi} = \sum_{n} E_n \left| c_n \right|^2 .
\end{equation}

The revision markup works here as well: \sh{author edit},
\ch{collaborator edit}, \rev{reviewer response}, \remove{deletion}.

\subsection{Four heading levels}
\label{sec:notes-levels}

\subsubsection{The third}

\subsubsubsection{The fourth, numbered}

\texttt{article} stops numbering at the third and sets \verb|\paragraph| as a
run-in head, so the preamble adds \verb|\subsubsubsection| and moves
\verb|\paragraph| down to a fifth level. The table of contents still stops at
the third.

\subsection{Text under review}
\label{sec:notes-draft}

\begin{draft}
What is inside a \texttt{draft} environment is set in this colour, with a mark
in the margin where it begins and a rule where it ends, and the colour reaches
floats and their captions as well. Only the author takes the environment off, so
what has not been reviewed is visible in the PDF and not only in the diff.

\begin{table}[H]
  \centering
  \caption{A table inside a draft block, which is coloured with it.}
  \begin{tabular}{lr}
    \toprule
    quantity & value \\
    \midrule
    one      & 1     \\
    two      & 2     \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}
\end{draft}
